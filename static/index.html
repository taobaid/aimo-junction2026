FROM INTENT TO A REVIEWABLE ORDER

How Trusted Trade works

  1. 01
    Describe the need

    Search a product, plan a meal or speak your request. Add quantities, preferences and a delivery date.

  2. 02
    Your agent does the sourcing

    It matches catalogue products, converts quantities and checks fixed prices and delivery availability.

  3. 03
    Review only what matters

    See the total, exceptions and proposed recipe changes. Spending above your authority and recipe changes need human approval.

  4. 04
    Confirm a mock order

    Prices, stock and permissions are rechecked. A receipt and decision history are saved. No supplier order or payment is sent.

Built for external purchasing agentsProduct search, offers, proposals and permission checks are available as structured APIs. The dashboard is the buyer’s view of that workflow.
